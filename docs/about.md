
# About Page Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
The About page establishes trust by telling Joshua Alvelo's story while reinforcing Innovative Twist Real Estate's education-first philosophy.

## Objectives
- Humanize the brand
- Build credibility
- Explain the company's mission
- Differentiate from transactional agents
- Encourage consultations

## Primary Audience
- Buyers
- Sellers
- Investors
- Property Owners
- Referral Partners

## Page Structure
1. Hero
2. Joshua's Story
3. Mission & Values
4. Why Clients Choose Innovative Twist
5. Credentials
6. Community Involvement
7. Meet the Team
8. Preferred Partners
9. CTA
10. Footer

## Hero
Headline:
Real Estate Built on Relationships, Education, and Trust.

Primary CTA:
Schedule a Consultation

Secondary CTA:
Explore Our Services

## Mission
Help families and investors make confident real estate decisions through education, transparency, and long-term wealth building.

## Core Values
- Education First
- Integrity
- Communication
- Innovation
- Long-Term Relationships

## Functional Requirements
- CMS editable content
- Team member cards
- Timeline component
- Embedded introduction video
- Testimonials carousel

## SEO

Joshua Alvelo
Innovative Twist Real Estate

## Analytics
Track:
- CTA clicks
