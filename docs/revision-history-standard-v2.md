# Revision History Standard
Version: 2.0

## Canonical References
Terminology in revision summaries must remain consistent with canonical-reference.md.

## Purpose
Provide a consistent revision history format for every specification.

## Required Format
| Version | Date | Author | Summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | YYYY-MM-DD | Joshua/AI | Initial specification |

## Versioning Rules
- Major: breaking structural or behavioral change
- Minor: additive backward-compatible change
- Patch: clarification or correction
