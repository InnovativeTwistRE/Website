# Market Reports PRD
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Generate localized market reports for buyers, sellers, investors, and homeowners.

## Report Types
- Monthly Market Update
- Community Snapshot
- Pricing Trends
- Inventory Trends
- Days on Market
- Investor Market Summary

## Features
- Dynamic charts
- Historical comparisons
- PDF export
- Scheduled delivery
- Email subscription

## AI
Summarize key changes and explain their practical impact.

## Acceptance Criteria
Reports are generated by the Reporting Engine and archived with version history.
