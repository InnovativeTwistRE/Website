# WealthScore™ PRD
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Provide a proprietary scoring model that summarizes the financial strength and long-term wealth potential of a property.

## Inputs
- Equity
- Cash Flow
- Appreciation
- Debt
- Occupancy
- Maintenance Risk
- Market Factors

## Outputs
- Overall WealthScore
- Risk Rating
- Opportunity Rating
- Recommended Actions

## Reporting
Results integrate with Investor Dashboard™, Homeowner Portal™, and Property Wealth Management™.

## AI
Explain why the score changed and identify the highest-impact improvement opportunities.

## Acceptance Criteria
Scoring model is versioned, transparent, and reproducible.
