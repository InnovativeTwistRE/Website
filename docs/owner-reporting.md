# Owner Reporting PRD
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Deliver professional reports that keep owners informed about property performance.

## Reports
- Monthly Statement
- Income & Expense Summary
- Maintenance Summary
- Inspection Summary
- Occupancy Report
- Lease Expiration Report

## Delivery
- Portal
- Email
- PDF
- Historical archive

## AI
Generate executive summaries highlighting trends, risks, and recommendations.

## Acceptance Criteria
Reports are generated by the Reporting Engine, versioned, and accessible only to authorized owners and staff.
