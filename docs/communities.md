# Communities Page Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version: 1.0

> This specification adds a public community-discovery experience without modifying the existing website or data architecture.

---

# Purpose

The Communities section helps users explore Hampton Roads and surrounding service areas by lifestyle, housing type, price range, amenities, and local character.

The experience should educate users about where they may want to live without making unsupported claims about schools, safety, demographics, or protected classes.

---

# Goals

- Improve local SEO
- Support buyer and relocation journeys
- Generate community-specific leads
- Connect neighborhoods to available properties
- Provide lifestyle-oriented educational content

---

Initial coverage may include:

- Virginia Beach
- Norfolk
- Chesapeake
- Portsmouth
- Suffolk
- Hampton
- Newport News
- Yorktown
- Poquoson

# Filters

- City
- Property Type
- Price Range
- Waterfront
- Golf Course
- New Construction
- 55+
- Urban
- Suburban
- Rural
- Commute Preference
- Nearby Amenities

All filters must avoid discriminatory steering.

- Mapping provider

---

# Functional Requirements

- Searchable directory
- Interactive map
- Community comparison
- Saved communities
- Listing integration
- Shareable URLs
- CMS-managed descriptions
- Dynamic market data
- Responsive layouts

---

# Compliance Requirements

Do not rank or describe communities using:

- Racial composition
- Religion
- Familial status
- Disability
- National origin
- Subjective safety claims
- Preferred resident type
- School quality claims without neutral third-party data and proper attribution

Use neutral, factual descriptions.

---

# AI Features

AI may:

- Recommend communities based on stated lifestyle preferences
- Summarize factual community information
- Build relocation checklists
- Compare objective attributes

AI must not infer protected characteristics or steer users based on them.

---

# Analytics

Track:

- Search filters
- Community views
- Saved communities
- Listing clicks
- Comparison usage
- Consultation submissions
- Map interactions

---

# SEO

Each community page should include:

- Unique title
- Unique description
- LocalBusiness or Place schema where appropriate
- Breadcrumbs
- Internal links
- Current market-data timestamp
- Canonical URL

---

# Acceptance Criteria

- Community content is factual and compliant
- Listings and market data show freshness dates
- Filters work on desktop and mobile
- Map is keyboard accessible
- Community pages connect to CRM and consultation workflows
- Pages support CMS editing and scheduled review
