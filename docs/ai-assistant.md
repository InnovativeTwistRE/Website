# AI Assistant PRD
Version: 1.0

## Purpose
Provide context-aware assistance throughout the platform.

## Responsibilities
- Answer questions
- Explain reports
- Recommend resources
- Summarize documents
- Guide workflows
- Surface next actions

## Guardrails
- Never fabricate data
- Cite platform sources when available
- Respect permissions
- Log AI interactions

## Supported Areas
- Website
- CRM
- Property Management
- Homeowner Portal™
- Investor Dashboard™
- Knowledge Center™

## Acceptance Criteria
Responses are permission-aware, auditable, and grounded in approved platform data.
