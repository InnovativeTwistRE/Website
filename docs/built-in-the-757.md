# Built in the 757™ Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version: 1.0

> This document defines the Built in the 757™ media experience as part of the broader Innovative Twist Real Estate platform.

---

# Purpose

Built in the 757™ is a local media brand featuring the people, businesses, builders, stories, and culture shaping the Hampton Roads region.

It should feel editorial and documentary-driven rather than like a traditional real estate blog.

---

# Goals

- Build local authority
- Strengthen community relationships
- Support the podcast and magazine
- Generate cross-channel audience growth
- Create sponsorship opportunities
- Introduce viewers to the Innovative Twist brand naturally

---
